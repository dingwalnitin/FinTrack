## 3. Domain / business-rule changes

### P12 — Credit cards

- **Model as liability account + card-specific facts** — `CreditCard` is a separate row referencing `Account.id` (1:1) so the authoritative balance container stays on `Account`. The card row carries the credit limit, statement cycle, due-date rule and reward balance; `accountType` must be `CREDIT_CARD` or `OTHER_LIABILITY` on registration.
- **Card transaction states** — `CardLineStatus` = `PENDING | POSTED | REVERSED | ADJUSTED`. A PENDING line MUST NOT carry a `transactionId`; a POSTED line is allowed to. The same swipe arriving twice (pending SMS then posted SMS) is collapsed at the data layer via the `lineIdentity` unique index.
- **Funding instrument explicit** — `CardPayment.fundingAccountId` is required and must differ from the `cardId`. The payment is structurally a settlement; rail analytics (`kind != TRANSFER` filter) already in place from P11 ensures payments are never double-counted as expenses.
- **Statement cycles / pending vs posted / snapshot** — `CardStatement` is a snapshot with `periodStart`/`periodEnd`/`dueDate` and a `statementIdentity` hash on `(cardId | periodStart | periodEnd)`. `totalDueMinor` is required, `minDueMinor` is informational and is allowed to be `null` (= unknown, never guessed).
- **Limits, utilization, account-level metrics** — `CreditCard.creditLimitMinor` is nullable. Downstream utilization MUST surface "unknown" when the limit is null (no fabrication in the model or the service).
- **Reward points / cashback as distinct benefit events** — `RewardEvent` carries `kind` ∈ {`CASHBACK`, `REWARD_POINTS`, `VOUCHER`, `OTHER`} and `classification` ∈ {`BENEFIT`, `REFUND`}. The service hard-codes BENEFIT; refund classification is reserved for explicit refund flows. Cashback on a credit card is BENEFIT, not a refund, and reduces the amount owed.
- **Statement adjustments** — `CardStatementAdjustment` is a first-class event linked to a statement. `kind` ∈ {`LATE_FEE`, `INTEREST`, `FEE_REVERSAL`, `GOODWILL_CREDIT`, `OTHER`}. DEBIT adds to the bill; CREDIT reduces it. Original transactions are never mutated.
- **Min vs total due** — `minDueMinor` is validated to be ≤ `totalDueMinor`; null is allowed (and surfaced as unknown).

### P13 — EMI plans

- **Plan model** — `EmiPlan` carries the funding `emiAccountId`, the merchant/biller and reference, plus optional `principalMinor` / `interestRateAnnualBps` / `installmentAmountMinor` / `totalInstallments` / `startDate`. Every numeric field can be `null` (= unknown). Status: `ACTIVE | CLOSED | PRECLOSED | REFINANCED | PAUSED`.
- **Detection / matching** — installment matching is keyed on the stable `(planId, installmentNumber)` unique index plus a `*Identity` hash on `(planId, installmentNumber)`. Re-running the matcher for the same logical installment is a no-op; ambiguous matches are not auto-merged.
- **Progress / payoff view** — `EmiPlanService.progress` returns paid / partial / missed counts, remaining installments, paid-minor and outstanding-principal-minor, plus a `Coverage` struct that explicitly flags which facts are evidenced vs unknown. The service NEVER fabricates missing evidence; if no installment row exists for a given month, the month is invisible to "paid" counts (the matcher marks `MISSED` only when the caller observes a missed due date).
- **Preclosure (module 147)** — `EmiPreclosure` is an explicit event with `kind` ∈ {`FORECLOSURE`, `SETTLEMENT`, `BUYOUT`}, evidenced outstanding, fee and adjustment amounts. The plan transitions to `PRECLOSED` with `closedAt` set. Historical installments are preserved.
- **Refinancing (module 147)** — the previous plan transitions to `REFINANCED`; a new `ACTIVE` plan is created with `refinancedFromPlanId` pointing to the old plan. A double-refinance on the same plan is rejected.
- **Missed / partial evidence** — `markInstallmentMissed` only sets `MISSED` when no payment exists. `recordInstallmentPayment` with `amountPaidMinor < amountDueMinor` sets `PARTIAL`; equal sets `PAID`. Both transitions require a `transactionId`; `MISSED` is independent of any transaction.
- **Completed-plan archive** — completed (PRECLOSED / CLOSED / REFINANCED) plans remain queryable for the lifetime of the database. The schema preserves them; the UI can archive a plan without losing history.

## 5. Acceptance-gate results

### P12

- ✅ Swipe, contactless, card-funded UPI are all `CardStatementLine` rows distinguished by `rail = CARD_POS / CARD_POS / UPI_ON_CARD`; the same swipe can land twice (PENDING then POSTED) and is collapsed at the data layer by `lineIdentity`.
- ✅ Card statement is opened with `periodStart`/`periodEnd`/`dueDate`; total due, min due and per-line amounts are surfaced; min ≤ total invariant enforced at construction.
- ✅ Card payment is a settlement: it carries `fundingAccountId` distinct from `cardId`; the service creates no `kind=EXPENSE` event. Re-runs with identical inputs are no-ops.
- ✅ Late-fee, interest, fee-reversal, goodwill-credit adjustments are explicit events linked to a statement; original transactions are never mutated.
- ✅ Min vs total due and due-date context are explicit; `minDueMinor` may be `null` (unknown, never guessed).
- ✅ Reward events are BENEFIT-classified by default; CASHBACK / VOUCHER require a positive amount; REWARD_POINTS require a `pointsDelta`. The cashback-vs-refund line is enforced by `classification` and the absence of a refund link.
- ✅ Limits, utilization, account-level metrics: `creditLimitMinor` is nullable; the model and the service NEVER fabricate a value.
- ✅ Realistic ambiguous / conflicting fixture (refund + cashback + dispute SMS arriving for the same order) is handled without auto-merge; the refund is a distinct row, the cashback is a distinct BENEFIT row, and the dispute is left for downstream REVIEW (the service does not invent a chargeback).

### P13

- ✅ Ordinary EMI plan is opened with `EmiPlanService.openPlan`; the first installment can be created atomically in the same call.
- ✅ Same-amount competing plans on the same account are independent; the matcher keys on `(planId, installmentNumber)`, not on amount or counterparty.
- ✅ Preclosure is an explicit event with evidenced outstanding / fee / adjustment amounts; the plan transitions to `PRECLOSED`; historical installments are preserved.
- ✅ Refinancing transitions the previous plan to `REFINANCED` and creates a new `ACTIVE` plan with `refinancedFromPlanId` set; a double-refinance is rejected.
- ✅ Missed/partial evidence is recorded explicitly; `markInstallmentMissed` is a no-op on a PAID installment; partial payment marks the installment PARTIAL.
- ✅ Progress is computed over a plan + its installments with explicit `Coverage` flags: every unknown field stays unknown; missing-evidence months are not invented as paid.
